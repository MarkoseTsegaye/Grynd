Grynd — Splits section rework

The splits list, the split editor, and the sheets they open. The editor is the weak point: its add-form occupies the top third of the screen permanently, its two attribute controls both offer a button labelled “Regular”, and both defaults are painted in full accent — so the untouched defaults shout louder than the actual primary action.

Two buttons named “Regular”Unilateral/Regular stacked above Plate loaded/Regular. Identical labels, and you can only tell them apart by reading the button to the left. Replaced with labelled segmented controls whose options name themselves.
Accent on every defaultBoth “Regular” defaults render in full accent lime — the primary CTA colour — while the real action (add) is a small square. Only a deliberate, non-default choice is tinted now.
Add form on demandCollapsed to one dashed “Add exercise” row; the form appears when you tap it. The list is the screen, not the form.
Rows that say somethingSplits carry last-performed and cycle usage; exercises carry their attributes and last working set, so both lists preview rather than just label.
Bare ✕ deletes instantlyThe editor removes an exercise with no confirmation, via a raw glyph that doesn’t match the trash icon used one screen earlier. Now swipe-to-reveal with a confirm.
Rename exists but is unreachablerenameSplit is in the store with no UI. The editor title becomes an editable field.
